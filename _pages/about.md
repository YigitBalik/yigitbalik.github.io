I am a PhD student at <a href="https://www.aalto.fi/en"> Aalto University</a> (Espoo, Finland) working with <a href="https://users.ics.aalto.fi/harrila/">Prof. Harri Lähdesmäki</a> as a part of the <a href="https://research.cs.aalto.fi/csb/"> Computational Systems Biology Research Group</a>.
My research area focuses on developing scalable and interpretable modern deep generative models for dynamical processes, such as high-dimensional time series data and single-cell data.

I have completed my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at <a href="https://www.itu.edu.tr/en/homepage"> Istanbul Technical University</a> where I worked with <a href="basira-lab.com"> Prof. Islem Rekik</a> on federated graph neural networks and with <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/oksuzilkay/"> Prof. Ilkay Oksuz</a> on interpretable deep learning for ECG analysis. 

I have completed my Master's degree in Computer Science with a major in Machine Learning at Aalto University.
